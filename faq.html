Frequently asked questions

Why was my vehicle booted?

Your vehicle was parked in violation of the posted rules on private property. The property owner has authorized JLS Boots to enforce parking with a vehicle boot instead of towing.

How do I get the boot removed?

Go to Boot Release, pick the number printed on your boot, and pay by card. Your release code appears right after payment. Enter the code into the lock and remove the boot yourself.

Where do I find my boot number?

It is printed on the front of the boot and on the notice left on your windshield.

How long do I have to return the boot?

hours from the time you pay. Drop it at any of our drop-off locations.

What happens if I don't return the boot?

A one-time replacement fee is charged to the card you paid with. Unpaid balances may be sent to collections.

What if I don't pay to remove the boot?

Vehicles left booted may be towed and impounded at the owner's expense, which costs more than a boot release.

Can I drive with the boot on?

No. Driving with the boot attached will damage your vehicle and the boot, and you may be responsible for the damage.
